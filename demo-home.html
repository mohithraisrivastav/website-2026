Lived Space: Creative Observation

Most creative education teaches us how to make. Lived Space explores how we see.

At the heart of every photograph, film, design, writing or artwork is observation. What we create depends on what we notice.

This workshop is a practice of paying closer attention. To light and shadow. To texture and material. To movement, behaviour, and atmosphere. To the relationships between people and space.

As observation deepens, creative work often becomes more personal, more intentional, and more distinct. Participants learn to slow down, look beyond the obvious, and develop their own way of seeing.

The goal is not to teach a technique. The goal is to strengthen attention. Because before we decide how to make something, we must first learn to see what is there.

Option J — The Paper

A single floating sheet of paper. Cursor casts a soft light on the surface. Content reads like a precious printed document with marginalia.

A field workshop · Goa

A practice in
looking before
making.

This workshop is open to professionals and enthusiasts across creative fields. It is for anyone whose work begins with seeing — designers and architects who study a space before responding, photographers who watch before they shoot, writers who notice before they describe, sound artists who listen before they record.

Goa, India. One day. Twelve people. Held monthly.

Sessions include the Explorer's Deck, field guidance during observation, and group review. Travel, accommodation and meals are not included. Bring your medium, comfortable clothing for outdoors, and a willingness to observe before you make.

What you leave with

iThe constraint card you drew. Use it again, anywhere.
iiYour workbook and journal. A space to continue observing.
iiiThe work you made — photographs, recordings, writing, sketches, video.
ivA practice you can repeat. Same three steps. Any space. Any day.

The One-Day Immersion

A single space, six to eight hours. You will move through all three steps and leave with one finished piece in your own medium.

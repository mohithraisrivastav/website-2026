The practice
Included

All workshop sessions. Explorer's Deck. Field guidance during observation. Group review after every session.

Not Included

Travel. Accommodation. Meals. Personal equipment or materials for your medium.

What To Bring

Your medium. Comfortable clothing for extended time outdoors. A willingness to observe before you make.

For whom

A practice for those whose work begins with seeing. For designers and architects who study a space before responding to its brief. For photographers and filmmakers who watch before they shoot. For writers who notice before they describe. For sound artists who listen before they record. For anyone who watches.

What you leave with
The constraint card you drew.
The practice continues anywhere you take it.
Your workbook and journal.
A place to return to, and notice how your eye sharpens.
The work you made.
Photographs, recordings, writing, sketches, video.
A practice you can repeat.
Same three steps. Any space. Any day.
The format

A single space. Six to eight hours. One finished piece.

You will move through all three steps — observation, constraint, output — and leave with one finished work in your own medium.

An image that returns to its source.

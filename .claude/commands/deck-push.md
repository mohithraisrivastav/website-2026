## Section 4 — Instagram organic captions (3 posts)

Write 3 captions. Each is one idea. No idea bleeds into the next.

**Post 1 — One card in practice**
Name a specific card type (what it asks someone to do). Then describe one real situation where a person would use it. Not "you'll notice space differently." The situation itself shows that.

## Voice Guardian Review (mandatory before output)

Check every email, DM, and caption. All pass or nothing is presented.

**Hard rule violations:**
1. Em dash or en dash present? Remove. Recast with a comma, period, or restructure.
2. Credential parade in email body? Remove entirely.
3. Caption ends with a moral or tells the reader what they will feel? Cut the last line.
4. Stacked fragments? Fold into one sentence.
5. Single-word or phrase lines for dramatic effect? Fold back into the sentence.
6. Repetition ladder? Fold into one sentence.
7. Claims about what the deck "will do" for someone? Replace with a fact about what the deck is or what someone actually does with it. Show use, not promised outcome.
8. "Not X, but Y" construction? Rewrite toward what it is.
9. Banned word? Replace. Banned: leverage, proactive, pivotal, seamless, stunning, breathtaking, vibrant, wonderful, incredible, transform, deepen, realm, quest, embark, game-changer, dynamic interplay, elevate, tapestry, journey.

**Quality check:**
10. Feeling described instead of scene shown? "It will change how you see space" is banned. Rewrite as what someone does with a card: "One of the cards asks you to photograph the same surface at three different times of day. Most people find they can't stop at three."
11. Can a reader picture it? If they are only getting a mood, go back and name the thing.
12. Does the writing sound like Mohith talking to a close friend — specific, unhurried, never performing? If it sounds like a product description: rewrite.
13. Two final tests: (1) Does this sound like Mohith? (2) Will the reader move through this without effort? If either is no: rewrite.

Do not present until all thirteen pass for every piece of content.
